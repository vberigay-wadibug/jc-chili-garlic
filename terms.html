Terms & Conditions

Orders

Orders are submitted through the website and confirmed by J&C through WhatsApp. An order is not considered finally accepted until availability, delivery details, and payment arrangements are confirmed.

Pricing

The displayed price is ₱150 per 250g jar. Standard delivery is ₱50. Orders with a subtotal of ₱700 or more receive free delivery, subject to final delivery confirmation.

Payment

Available methods shown on the order page are Cash on Delivery (COD), GCash, and Maya. Payment instructions for non-COD orders will be provided directly by J&C after the order is submitted.

Customer information

Customers should provide accurate contact and delivery information. J&C may contact the customer through the provided mobile number or WhatsApp to confirm the order.

Order changes and cancellation

Request changes or cancellation as soon as possible through WhatsApp. Once an order has been prepared or dispatched, changes may no longer be possible.

Contact

For questions about an order, contact J&C Chili Garlic through the details on the Contact page.
